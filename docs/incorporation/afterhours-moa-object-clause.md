# Afterhours Private Limited — MOA Object Clause

> Draft for company registration in India (Companies Act, 2013). Hand to CA/CS for filing.
> This is general drafting help, not legal advice. Have a Company Secretary or CA finalise before filing with MCA/ROC.

## III. (A) The objects to be pursued by the Company on its incorporation are:

**1.** To design, develop, own and operate internet, mobile and software platforms that enable users to discover, create, host, promote, manage and attend social events and experiences; and to provide ticketing, registration, booking and event-management services, including facilitating the sale of tickets and the collection of payments on behalf of hosts and organisers, and earning commissions, service fees and other charges for doing so.

**2.** To develop, deploy and operate identity, age, gender and user-authentication verification technologies, including artificial-intelligence and biometric systems, in order to verify user eligibility and maintain safety and trust on the Company's platforms, and to operate gender-specific and women-only platforms, communities and services that admit users on the basis of such verification.

**3.** To build and operate social networking, community and communication platforms that connect individuals and groups, and to develop data-driven and artificial-intelligence systems for personalisation, recommendation, matchmaking and friend-finding among users.

**4.** To carry on the business of an online and offline marketplace and e-commerce platform, including in the fashion, apparel, lifestyle and consumer-goods categories and with a particular focus on (but not limited to) women-oriented products and services, for the listing, sale, purchase and fulfilment of goods, services, experiences and digital content, and to earn commissions, fees and margins from doing so.

**5.** To provide technology, software, data-analytics and artificial-intelligence products and services, and to earn revenue from the Company's platforms, content and data through advertising, sponsorship, subscriptions and other lawful commercial arrangements, in India and worldwide.

## III. (B) Matters incidental or ancillary

Standard boilerplate to be appended by the CA in his usual format (payment facilitation through licensed providers, IP ownership and licensing, JVs/investments/partnerships, advertising, borrowing powers, property, and acts incidental to the above).

## Notes / flags for the CA

1. **No fixed numbers in the charter.** The platform take-rate (~30% on ticket sales) is deliberately kept out of the MOA. Numbers in a charter are a liability and the take-rate will change. Keep it in the business plan only.

2. **Payments may trigger RBI regulation.** Object 1 involves collecting ticket money on behalf of hosts and taking a cut. Confirm settlements route through a licensed payment aggregator (Razorpay/Cashfree) so Afterhours never holds funds directly and avoids payment-aggregator regulation.

3. **Gender verification = sensitive personal data → DPDP Act, 2023.** AI/biometric gender verification (Object 2) processes sensitive personal data. Note consent obligations, handling of false rejections/appeals, and treatment of transgender users. The MOA wording covers the activity; compliance lives outside the MOA.

4. **Register tone.** "for doing so" replaced the legalese tails ("in respect thereof", "in connection therewith") for readability. The CA may revert these if an ROC reviewer expects the formal register. Substance is identical either way.

5. **Scope consistency.** Objects 1, 3, 5 describe an open, all-users platform; Object 2 introduces women-only. Kept broad for optionality. Make sure product positioning is clear internally on whether Afterhours is women-only end-to-end or only for certain communities/events.
